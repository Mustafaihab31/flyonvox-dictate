01 — What it does

Built for people who type with their voice.

Every feature exists because a real dictation session demanded it.

Hotkey from anywhere

One combo, system-wide. Press to start, press again to stop. The app quietly parks in your tray.

Pastes itself

Transcripts land in the active window — Slack, Notion, your terminal. Clipboard is restored after.

Local & offline

Whisper runs on your CPU or GPU. No data leaves the machine. Works on a plane, in a vault, in a basement.

Blazing fast

First words appear in under a second on a mid-range GPU. Long clips transcribe faster than realtime.

Many languages

English-only or multilingual Whisper models, switchable in one click. Pick the one that matches your voice.

Floating bubble

An always-on-top mic circle that turns into a live volume bar when you speak. Drag it anywhere, click to record.

02 — How it works

Three steps. Zero friction.

Designed to disappear into your workflow, not interrupt it.

  1. 01

    Press the hotkey

    A small floating bubble appears. The system chime is optional and gentle.

    Ctrl Alt Space
  2. 02

    Talk naturally

    Whisper transcribes on your machine as you speak. Pause, think, restart — it handles it.

  3. 03

    It pastes

    The transcript lands in whatever app had focus. Your clipboard is restored right after.

    Ctrl V → Pasted!
